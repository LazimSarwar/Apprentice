      How it works

      Four models, each with one job. Every verdict points back to something that was seen or said.

      Listen
      What was said
      Muse Voice Transcribe turns the narration into timed lines. Muse Spark ties each line to a step: reasons, critical steps, spoken order rules. Quotes are checked against the transcript.
      Understand
      Ordered steps
      Muse Spark reads each step and its result from the video, naming objects in the speaker's words. Also runs on the open-weight Muse Spark Open.
      Evidence
      Every object tracked
      SAM 3.1 follows each object frame by frame. Steps are checked against its boxes, and a step it couldn't see is reported as unverified, not as skipped.
      Judge
      Required, free, or as shown
      Each step is located in time; a temporal monitor checks only the orders that matter. Broken rules fail; unexplained differences are notes.
      Demo clips are generated test videos (flat shapes, computer-voiced narration); the robot is a simulated Franka Panda (MuJoCo). Verdicts and rates come straight from the pipeline and include every failure.
