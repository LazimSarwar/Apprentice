# OneShot packing trial — record before inspecting model results

```text
Use a box, a paper/cloth liner, one product, an accessory and a card. Prefer distinct
colors/shapes, except in the final identical-parts challenge. Avoid personal documents,
visible phone notifications, private addresses or faces in the recording.

REF.mp4 — participant A demonstrates a correct pack and says:
"Put the liner in the box first, before everything else, to protect the product.
The product goes on the liner. Put the accessory in the box and the card on the product.
The accessory and card can go in either order."
Show the final arrangement for two seconds. Record continuously, ideally 10–25 seconds.

Have a SECOND PERSON (anonymous code B) record these attempts:
P01.mp4 — correct; vary starting positions.
P02.mp4 — accessory before liner, then finish correctly. The final state should look right.
P03.mp4 — omit the card; show the workspace clearly.
P04.mp4 — leave the accessory beside the box, rather than inside.
P05.mp4 — put accessory inside correctly, then remove it and leave it beside the box.
P06.mp4 — cover the card's placement AND final position; do not reveal its result.
P07.mp4 — correct, with another camera angle and starting positions.
P08.mp4 — initially put accessory beside box, then correct it and finish correctly.
P09.mp4 — two same-color/same-shape accessories; make the instance identity ambiguous.

Predetermined labels:
P01 pass; P02 wrong order; P03 skipped step; P04 wrong place; P05 wrong place;
P06 review required; P07 pass; P08 pass (notes allowed); P09 review required.

A human must check the actual recording against each intended label before inference.
Record retakes and coaching. Do not choose only successful model outputs. Retain every
first submitted result, including errors and abstentions. Record participant codes, not names.

Keep the new clips in a folder outside this repository. Raw videos are never committed.
Use the app's "Packing trial" page: teach and confirm REF once, freeze the trial plan,
then submit the clips against their predeclared cases. Labels are not given to the model.

Optional impact measurement: record how long a human spends reviewing a clip manually,
and how long they spend inspecting the assisted result. Keep model waiting time separate.
Do not report time savings until actual paired measurements exist. These self-timed
figures are an exploratory pilot, not a controlled user study.
```
